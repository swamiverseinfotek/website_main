EXECUTIVE CONTEXT

Why Traditional Mobile Claim & Financial Apps Fail

Most mobile banking and insurance apps are built only for simple policy renewals, completely ignoring the complex legal, evidentiary, and multi-generational verification required for dormant asset and IEPF share recovery.

⚠️ The Mobile Asset Recovery Dilemma

In modern financial ecosystems, recovering ancestral shares, uncollected dividends, or contested policy payouts involves physical documentation and succession verification. Traditional mobile apps fail to address these realities:

  • ✕
    Physical Paperwork & Lost Folios: Claimants have to carry physical paper documents, share certificates, and death certificates to offline branch offices with zero mobile visibility.
  • ✕
    Missing Heirship & Lineage Tools: Proving legal heirship for deceased investor shares requires complex family trees that standard mobile banking apps cannot represent.
  • ✕
    Disconnected Channel Partners: Field agents and financial advisors lack mobile CRM tools to manage client leads, track case submissions, and monitor commissions on the go.
  • ✕
    Flaky Network & Server Cold-Starts: Enterprise cloud endpoints that spin down cause mobile apps to hang indefinitely on white screens, frustrating users in low-connectivity areas.

✦ The MyClaim Mobile Solution

MyClaim Mobile solves this by replacing manual legwork with a responsive, offline-tolerant, role-adaptive native mobile client:

  • ✓
    Dual-Role Dynamic Shells: Automatically routes authenticated users into either a dedicated Claimant Dashboard or a Partner Sales Suite based on JWT claims.
  • ✓
    Interactive Genealogical Family Tree: Directly map out multi-generational legal heirs on a touch canvas to assemble valid succession claims.
  • ✓
    Instant IEPF Share & Dividend Discovery: Query corporate registrars by company name or folio ID directly from the mobile handset.
  • ✓
    Hardware-Level Biometric Protection: Secures confidential PAN, Aadhaar, and share certificates behind device-native biometric sensors.
CORE ENGINEERING INNOVATIONS

What Makes MyClaim Mobile Feel Truly Enterprise-Grade

Six foundational architectural subsystems engineered to elevate MyClaim Mobile beyond generic banking apps into a native financial recovery powerhouse.

🔒

Device-Native Biometric Security Vault

Integrates hardware-level biometrics (local_auth) supporting Face ID, Fingerprint, and OS Passcode fallback. Features delayed auto-triggering for OEM screen transitions (Oppo ColorOS, Samsung OneUI) and kinetic shake animation feedback on failed attempts.

🌳

Interactive Heirship & Family Tree Engine

A custom mobile genealogical tree manager backed by FamilyTreeProvider. Claimants can visually add nodes for spouses, siblings, and legal heirs on the go, producing structured heirship data critical for government IEPF verification.

👥

Role-Adaptive Dynamic Shell Routing

Uses indexed stack state shells (ClientShell and PartnerShell) to hot-swap navigation hierarchies seamlessly. Clients see portfolios and claim trackers, while Partners see leads, client directories, and commission statements.

⚡

Sleep-Resilient & Offline-Tolerant API Client

Engineered with a strict 4-second network timeout threshold. If hosted microservices are asleep or unreachable, ApiService gracefully falls back to cached offline state and mock datasets, ensuring zero UI freezing.

📑

Integrated Mobile Document Vault & Picker

Leverages native file_picker and image_picker bridges to let users capture physical share certificates, indemnity bonds, and KYC proofs directly via mobile camera or PDF import into a multipart upload pipeline.

🔔

Native Event & Notification Engine

Powered by flutter_local_notifications for system-tray eventing. Users receive real-time push and local alerts whenever case stages advance, documents require re-upload, or partner support tickets are resolved.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

End-to-End Topology Blueprint

Full data flow tracing biometric verification, role resolution, provider state synchronization, and secure REST communication.

1

App Initialization & Animated Splash Boot

On boot, main.dart initializes ScreenUtilInit (390×844 dp baseline) and NotificationService. SplashScreen executes multi-controller particle, pulse, and ripple animations while verifying versioned onboarding flags.

Splash Engine
2

Hardware Biometric Authentication & Session Recovery

If an existing session exists in SharedPreferences, BiometricLockScreen engages device-native biometric sensors. Upon biometric confirmation, the user's JWT credentials unlock the app.

local_auth Gate
3

Role-Based Dynamic Shell Routing

AuthProvider evaluates the stored role. Clients are routed to ClientShell (home, claims, new claim hub, services, documents), while registered channel partners enter PartnerShell (partner metrics, leads, client book, tickets).

Dual Shell Router
4

Interactive Genealogical Heirship Tree Construction

In the quick action module, users tap into FamilyTreeScreen to map family lineages. Adding members dynamically calculates relationship hierarchies managed reactively by FamilyTreeProvider.

FamilyTreeProvider
5

Multi-Source Document Capture & Vault Sync

Users upload physical share certificates or KYC records via camera or filesystem. Files are validated for size/type and streamed to /api/documents/upload with local status tracking.

Multipart Pipeline
6

Background Push Alerts & Real-Time Case Progression

When claim milestones change or partner tickets are resolved, NotificationService fires local notification channels with status badges, prompting users directly into relevant case views.

NotificationService
