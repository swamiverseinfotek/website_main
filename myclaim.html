EXECUTIVE CONTEXT

Why Traditional Claim & Asset Recovery Fails

Most claimants and wealth investors face months of bureaucratic delays, lost physical paperwork, and opaque multi-agency tracking when attempting to recover unclaimed dividends, IEPF shares, and insurance settlements.

⚠️ The Financial Recovery Dilemma

In modern financial ecosystems, recovering dormant securities, IEPF dividends, and settling contested insurance claims involves navigating complex multi-stakeholder chains. Traditional recovery workflows face structural bottlenecks:

  • ✕
    Fragmented Partner-Client Chains: Independent financial advisors and regional channel partners operate on fragmented spreadsheets, causing lost leads and disconnected client histories.
  • ✕
    Manual Document Processing Bottlenecks: KYC verification, share certificates, and indemnity bonds require days of manual human data entry, introducing frequent clerical errors.
  • ✕
    Opaque Status Black-Holes: Claimants have zero real-time visibility into whether documents are submitted, under legal review, or filed with regulatory authorities.
  • ✕
    Unstructured Ticket & Case Escalations: Communication between operations personnel, legal underwriters, and field agents relies on disparate emails and messaging apps with no SLA accountability.

✦ The MyClaim Unified Portal

MyClaim solves this by replacing disjointed offline paperwork with an interconnected, real-time digital ecosystem designed for scale, transparency, and compliance:

  • ✓
    Multi-Tier Hierarchical Portals: Role-segregated environments for Super Admins, Admins, Super Partners, Partners, Clients, and Operations Employees.
  • ✓
    In-Browser OCR & Instant Document Classification: Tesseract.js extraction validates certificates, PAN cards, and identity documents instantly on upload.
  • ✓
    Duplex Real-Time Synchronization: Socket.io event channels broadcast ticket updates, claim progress, and notifications with zero manual page refreshes.
  • ✓
    Automated Legal Dossiers & Exportable Reports: jsPDF and html2canvas generate standardized, tamper-resistant claim dossiers, proposals, and IEPF audit reports on demand.
CORE ENGINEERING INNOVATIONS

What Makes MyClaim Feel Truly Enterprise-Grade

Six foundational architectural subsystems engineered to elevate MyClaim beyond generic CRM software into an enterprise-grade financial operations portal.

🎨

Enterprise Form Animation & Progressive Reveal

Engineered with custom motion utilities, floating labels, active-field spotlight effects, dynamic progress trackers, and 50+ keyframes to prevent visual fatigue across complex, multi-step financial onboarding forms without relying on bulky external UI kits.

⚡

Real-Time Socket.io Duplex Event Bus

Bi-directional WebSocket streaming keeps dashboards, operational queues, and client timelines synchronized. Status changes, ticket replies, and file approvals update across distributed user sessions in real time.

🔍

In-Browser Tesseract.js OCR Pipeline

Eliminates backend processing bottlenecks by executing optical character recognition directly within the client browser. Extracts folio numbers, company names, and certificate IDs before payload transmission.

👥

Multi-Tier Hierarchical Channel Architecture

Models real-world financial distribution channels with Super Partner and Partner relationships. Features automated referral tracking, commission structures, and segregated client book views backed by strict Mongoose permission scopes.

📄

Client-Side Dossier & PDF Generation

Enables instantaneous generation of client proposals, IEPF recovery requests, and operational summaries using jsPDF and html2canvas directly in the browser, eliminating heavy server-side PDF rendering delays.

🤖

Integrated CrewAI Multi-Agent Autonomous Workforce

Backed by an autonomous Python agent crew (claim_agent, document_agent, employee_agent, finance_agent, notification_agent) capable of triaging missing documentation, calculating financial summaries, and routing requests dynamically.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

End-to-End Topology Blueprint

Full data flow tracing document uploads, client-side OCR extraction, bi-directional socket eventing, role-based authorization, and agentic validation.

1

Client Onboarding & Dynamic Form Progression

The applicant or channel partner navigates a guided multi-step form powered by EnhancedFormInput and useFormAnimations. Active field spotlights guide input while animated step trackers validate inputs progressively.

Motion UI Engine
2

Local Document Processing & Optical Verification

Uploaded identity proofs, share certificates, and policy documents enter the DocumentUploadZone. Tesseract.js performs client-side OCR extraction, pre-filling verified folio numbers and company metadata.

In-Browser OCR
3

Real-Time Ingestion & WebSocket Broadcast

Payloads post to the Node.js/Express API. Upon record persistence, the Socket.io server pushes synchronized notification events to the relevant partner, assigned case employee, and administrative queues.

Socket.io Event Bus
4

Role-Segregated Operational Routing

Claims, leads, and service tickets are classified into designated operational departments (IEPF Recovery, Pre-IPO Allocation, Wealth Management, or Policy Disputes) with departmental permission gates.

Mongoose Scopes
5

CrewAI Agent Triage & Validation

The background CrewAI autonomous agent harness queries the database via custom tools (GetClaimSummary, GetMissingDocuments, GetFinancialSummary) to audit filing readiness and flag missing evidentiary documents.

CrewAI Multi-Agent
6

Exportable Legal Dossiers & Payout Reconciliation

Once verified, the portal compiles a formatted case dossier using jsPDF. Clients and partners receive immutable downloadable PDF summaries, and finance routes log commission and settlement tallies.

Tamper-Proof jsPDF
