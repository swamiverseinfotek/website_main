1. EXECUTIVE CONTEXT

Why Commercial Transportation Needs ABHEDYA

Commercial vehicles transport valuable cargo across long distances, facing serious transit vulnerabilities where traditional monitoring solutions operate in disconnected silos.

⚠️ The Fragmented Monitoring Dilemma

Traditional monitoring solutions offer separate services for vehicle tracking, video recording, and security monitoring. This creates fragmented data, making it difficult to obtain a complete, reliable picture of an incident:

  • ✕
    Cargo Theft & Unauthorized Access: Inability to correlate vehicle telemetry with cargo compartment breaches in real time.
  • ✕
    Road Accidents & Unexpected Incidents: Critical transit events occur without a unified forensic record of speed, movement, and impact.
  • ✕
    Route Deviations & Unapproved Stops: Movement tracking lacks integration with onboard sensor alerts and driver context.
  • ✕
    Insurance Claim Investigation Friction: Missing or contested incident records prolong dispute resolution and claim payouts.
  • ✕
    Uncoordinated Standalone Systems: Multi-vendor hardware adds complexity, maintenance overhead, and data blind spots.

✦ The ABHEDYA Concept

Inspired by the flight data recorders (blackboxes) that safeguard aviation, ABHEDYA brings an equivalent incident-recording standard to commercial road transport:

  • ✓
    Centralized Incident Recording: Unifies telemetry, cargo status, and vehicle movement into a single dedicated hardware device.
  • ✓
    Continuous Event Evaluation: Onboard embedded intelligence evaluates telemetry continuously to identify unusual occurrences.
  • ✓
    Tamper-Evident Journey Logs: Records critical events in a protected digital log format for post-incident review.
  • ✓
    Remote Situational Alerts: Supports remote notifications to keep dispatch teams and operators informed of anomalies.
  • ✓
    Objective Claim Verification: Delivers trustworthy digital proof that assists insurance adjusters and accident investigators.

4. HOW ABHEDYA WORKS

From Vehicle Monitoring to Incident Records

Operates as an intelligent monitoring and recording device installed in commercial vehicles, following an end-to-end 6-stage lifecycle.

1. Monitor

Continuously observes vital vehicle, movement, and transportation activities across every leg of the journey.

2. Collect

Gathers relevant multi-sensory telemetry throughout transit to build a cohesive operational data baseline.

3. Process

Evaluates incoming operational information on-device to rapidly detect unusual occurrences, impacts, or boundary breaches.

4. Record

Preserves important event-related records in a dedicated, tamper-evident digital format for long-term accountability.

5. Notify

Supports remote communication of relevant alerts to fleet supervisors and emergency dispatch personnel.

6. Review

Makes recorded information readily available for post-incident investigation, insurance verification, and safety auditing.
